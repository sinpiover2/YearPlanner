## Unit 1 - Rigid Transformations and Congruence

- **Source label:** 8.1 Unit Overview
- **Required Days:** Confirmed absent
- **Optional Days:** Confirmed absent

**Overview (publisher text):**

Students learn to visualize, describe, and perform translations, rotations, and reflections on a grid. They learn and use the word congruent and use rigid transformations to determine whether figures are congruent. Students also apply rigid transformations to discover new angle relationships (e.g., that corresponding angles on parallel lines are congruent and that the sum of the interior angle measures in a triangle is 180°).

### Unit at a Glance

- **Explore: Tessellations** - `(Optional)` Launch the unit with a non-routine task to investigate the question, "How can patterns be created using shapes?"
- **Sub-Unit 1: Rigid Transformations** `(Lessons 1-6 + Practice Day + Quiz)` - Perform translations, reflections, and rotations on and off a grid.
- **Sub-Unit 2: Congruence** `(Lessons 7-9)` - Determine whether two figures are congruent using rigid transformations.
- **Sub-Unit 3: Angle Relationships** `(Lessons 10-13 + Practice Day)` - Use rigid transformations to discover new angle relationships and determine angle measures.

The overview does not include `Getting to Know Each Other`, the `8.1 Pre-Unit Check`, the `8.1 Performance Task`, or `Unit Synthesis and Reflection` in its sub-unit ranges. Their pages occur in the publisher's Unit 1 sequence, and they are included below without assigning them to a sub-unit that the source does not assign.

### Material Ambiguities and Unresolved Questions

1. `Getting to Know Each Other` is visibly optional and is called "this lesson" in supporting prose, but no separate Type label is present. Type remains **Confirmed absent** rather than being inferred as `Lesson` or normalized to IM1's `Meet & Greet`.
2. The `8.1 Pre-Unit Check` may be completed before the unit or spread throughout it. This is flexibility in administration, not a publisher-flexible sequence item comparable to a source that explicitly has no fixed position; the card retains its fixed source position.
3. `End-of-Unit Assessment` appears in the supporting descriptions for `8.1 Practice Day 2` and `Unit Synthesis and Reflection`. No distinct card or item with that Type/title exists in the complete Unit 1 source. It is therefore not extracted as a separate instructional item. Whether it is publisher shorthand for the visible `8.1 Performance Task` is **not stated** and remains unresolved.
4. `Unit Synthesis and Reflection` contains six optional internal activities and directs the teacher to select one to two. The source does not mark the enclosing item optional; item-level optionality is therefore **Confirmed absent**, distinct from its internal activity choice.
5. All 22 PDF pages are raster-image pages corresponding in order and dimensions to the 22 screenshots. The PDF has no text layer; transcription was visually validated against both representations.
