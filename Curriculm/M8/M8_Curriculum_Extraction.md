## Unit 1 - Rigid Transformations and Congruence

- **Source label:** 8.1 Unit Overview
- **Required Days:** Confirmed absent
- **Optional Days:** Confirmed absent

**Overview (publisher text):**

Students learn to visualize, describe, and perform translations, rotations, and reflections on a grid. They learn and use the word congruent and use rigid transformations to determine whether figures are congruent. Students also apply rigid transformations to discover new angle relationships (e.g., that corresponding angles on parallel lines are congruent and that the sum of the interior angle measures in a triangle is 180°).

### Unit at a Glance

- **Explore: Tessellations** - `(Optional)` Launch the unit with a non-routine task to investigate the question, "How can patterns be created using shapes?"
- **Sub-Unit 1: Rigid Transformations** `(Lessons 1-6 + Practice Day + Quiz)` - Perform translations, reflections, and rotations on and off a grid.
- **Sub-Unit 2: Congruence** `(Lessons 7-9)` - Determine whether two figures are congruent using rigid transformations.
- **Sub-Unit 3: Angle Relationships** `(Lessons 10-13 + Practice Day)` - Use rigid transformations to discover new angle relationships and determine angle measures.

## Unit 2 - Dilations, Similarity, and Slope

- **Source label:** 8.2 Unit Overview
- **Required Days:** Confirmed absent
- **Optional Days:** Confirmed absent

**Overview (publisher text):**

Students study dilations and similar figures. They perform dilations on and off the coordinate plane using a scale factor and center of dilation. Students use dilations and previously learned transformations to justify that two triangles are similar. Using similar triangles, students learn about the concept of slope triangles, which help them determine the slope of a given line.

### Unit at a Glance

- **Explore: Standard Paper Sizes** - `(Optional)` Launch the unit with a non-routine task to investigate the question, "Are all rectangles scaled copies of each other?"
- **Sub-Unit 1: Dilations** `(Lessons 1-5 + Practice Day + Quiz)`
  - Apply dilations to figures on and off a coordinate grid.
  - **Language Goal:** Describe dilations precisely using terms such as pre-image, center of dilation, and scale factor.
- **Sub-Unit 2: Similarity** `(Lessons 6-8)`
  - Use transformations to determine whether two figures are similar.
  - **Language Goal:** Justify whether two figures are similar using terms such as translation, reflection, rotation, dilation, pre-image, and image.
- **Sub-Unit 3: Slope** `(Lessons 9-10 + Practice Day)`
  - Make connections between slope and similar triangles on a line.
  - Determine the slopes of lines.
  - **Language Goal:** Explain how to calculate the slope of a line using vocabulary from this unit.

## Unit 3 - Proportional and Linear Relationships

- **Source label:** 8.3 Unit Overview
- **Required Days:** Confirmed absent
- **Optional Days:** Confirmed absent

**Overview (publisher text):**

Students make connections between proportional relationships and non-proportional linear relationships. They compare representations of linear relationships and interpret them in context. Students also determine solutions to two-variable linear equations of different forms.

### Unit at a Glance

- **Explore: Visual Patterns** - `(Optional)` Launch the unit with a non-routine task to investigate the question, "What patterns do you notice in shapes and numbers?"

- **Sub-Unit 2: Linear Relationships** `(Lessons 4-8 + Practice Day + Quiz)`

  - **Language Goal:** Interpret the vertical intercept and slope of a linear relationship in context.
- **Sub-Unit 3: Linear Equations and Points** `(Lessons 9-13 + Practice Day)`
  - Write an equation for a linear relationship given two points.
  - Make connections between graphs and solutions of two-variable linear equations.
  - **Language Goal:** Analyze strategies for calculating the slope and vertical intercept of a line through two points.

- **Source label:** 8.4 Unit Overview
- **Required Days:** Confirmed absent
- **Optional Days:** Confirmed absent

**Overview (publisher text):**

Students solve linear equations and determine the number of possible solutions. They also solve systems of two linear equations algebraically and graphically.

### Unit at a Glance

- **Explore: Equation Puzzles** - `(Optional)` Launch the unit with a non-routine task to investigate the question, "How is an equation like a puzzle?"
- **Sub-Unit 1: Solving Linear Equations** `(Lessons 1-8 + Practice Day + Quiz)`
  - Write and solve equations with variables on both sides of the equal sign.
  - Determine the number of solutions to a linear equation.
  - **Language Goal:** Analyze strategies for solving linear equations in one variable.
- **Sub-Unit 2: Solve Systems of Equations** `(Lessons 9-14 + Practice Day)`
  - Solve systems of linear equations in two variables graphically and algebraically.
  - **Language Goal:** Interpret the intersection point of two linear relationships in context given their graphs or equations.
  - **Language Goal:** Describe strategies for solving systems of linear equations.
